Why Maki?

Because you build AI close to the business and plan to grow the team. And because a distributor with more than 100 brands in four countries has exactly the many repetitive tasks where AI makes a noticeable difference.

Have you built AI solutions before?

Yes. HeyOtto is 21 agents in 3 teams that share the work in one chat, where everything waits for a human yes before it goes out. I have also built more than 300 product pages automatically from product data.

How do you work with IT?

IT sets the boundaries for platforms, security, data access and compliance, and I build within them. If a solution needs an integration or changes to an existing system, we do it together.

How do you make sure the solutions are used?

By building them together with the people who will use them, and by staying on after they go live. I train, document and follow up on usage, and I adjust when the feedback says something needs to change.

Which tools do you use?

Whatever fits the task and your boundaries. Typically Claude, n8n, Google Cloud, Google Sheets, GitHub, Netlify, Lovable and VS Code, plus APIs to connect AI with the data the solution needs.
